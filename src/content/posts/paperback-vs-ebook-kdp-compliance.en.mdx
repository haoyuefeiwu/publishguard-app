One of the most confusing moments for KDP publishers happens when the **Kindle eBook version goes Live**, while the **Paperback version** of the exact same manuscript gets blocked during Print Review.

## Why Paperback Review Is Stricter Than eBook Review

Paperback proofs go through visual thumbnail inspection:

1. **Zero Rectangular Box Layouts**: Avoid heavy bordered `.prompt-card` containers that make a serious non-fiction book look like a printable workbook in thumbnail previews.
2. **Exact Author-Cover Parity**: Your front cover, spine, copyright page, and metadata `Author` field must match character-for-character as a realistic human pen name—never use `"Team"`, `"Studio"`, or fictional publisher cities like `"New York · London"`.
3. **100% Pure Editorial Content**: Never include printable vouchers, discount codes (`WELCOME15`), or cut-out cards inside the interior pages.

## Pen-Name Domain Isolation & Weekly Cadence

Using the same companion website domain or publishing rapid-fire titles within 24 hours across multiple pen profiles increases batch-review risk. Space new title launches at least **7 days apart** per profile and isolate each pen identity with its own dedicated companion domain.
