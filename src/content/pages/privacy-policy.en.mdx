## 1. Introduction

At **PublishGuard** (`https://getpublishguard.com`), protecting your unpublished manuscripts, pen-name matrix, and personal data is foundational to our business. This Privacy Policy explains what we collect and how we keep it strictly isolated.

## 2. Zero Model-Training Guarantee on Your Manuscripts

- Your book outlines, chapters, pen names, companion domains, and uploaded EPUB files are processed **strictly to execute your requested compliance scans, prose generation, and PDF/EPUB compilation**.
- **We never sell, publish, or use your private manuscripts to train public or shared AI foundation models.**

## 3. Information We Collect

1. **Account Information**: Name, email address, and encrypted authentication credentials.
2. **Publishing Workspace Data**: Pen profiles, companion domains, book blueprints, chapter drafts, and compiled export bundles stored privately in your account.
3. **Billing & Payment Data**: Processed securely by our payment provider (Stripe). PublishGuard does not store full credit card numbers on our servers.
4. **Scanner Rate-Limiting Metadata**: Temporarily hashed IP identifiers to enforce daily anonymous limits on the free `/tools/kdp-guard` scanner.

## 4. Data Isolation & Security

Every pen profile and manuscript project is scoped strictly to your authenticated user ID. All sensitive platform credentials and API keys are encrypted at rest and transmitted over TLS.

## 5. Data Export & Deletion Rights

You may export your compiled books at any time or request complete deletion of your account and associated manuscripts by emailing **`support@getpublishguard.com`**.

## 6. Contact

For privacy or GDPR/CCPA data requests, contact **`support@getpublishguard.com`**.
